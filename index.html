👤 About

I am a PhD student at Inria and Grenoble INP, researching “Efficient task hybridization in heterogeneous computing: practical combinations of Control and Scheduling theories.” My work is part of the CTRL‑A project and aims to design adaptive control strategies for dynamic cloud and HPC environments.

Previously, I completed an MSc internship at Inria's Spirals team, focusing on control-theoretic optimization for compute-intensive systems. I hold an engineering degree from the National Polytechnic School of Algiers and currently live in Grenoble, France, where I combine control systems, smart automation, and large-scale computing research.
