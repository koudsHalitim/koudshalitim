👤 About

I am a passionate Automation Engineering student at the National Polytechnic School of Algiers, currently residing in Grenoble, France. My focus lies in control systems, robotics, and smart automation. I recently completed a research internship at INRIA's Spirals team, exploring control-theoretic strategies to improve efficiency in compute-intensive environments. I'm also driven by a deep interest in applying engineering principles to real-world innovation challenges.
