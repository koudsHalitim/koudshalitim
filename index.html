About

I am a final-year automation engineering student at the National Polytechnic School of Algiers, currently living in Grenoble, France. I specialize in control systems, robotics, and industrial process optimization. As an ambassador for the International Youth Math Challenge (IYMC), I also promote mathematical innovation among youth. My recent work includes an MSc internship at INRIA on control theory applied to compute-intensive applications.
