👤 About

I am a PhD candidate at Inria and Grenoble INP, focused on hybridizing task scheduling and control theory in high-performance computing systems. My thesis, titled “Efficient task hybridization in heterogeneous computing: practical combinations of Control and Scheduling theories”, addresses the development of adaptive, intelligent controllers for resource-constrained, compute-intensive platforms.

My academic path includes an MSc internship with Inria's Spirals team, where I explored control-based energy optimizations for parallel workloads. I previously earned my engineering degree from the National Polytechnic School of Algiers. Currently based in Grenoble, I work at the intersection of control theory, system architecture, and smart automation to contribute to the evolution of scalable and responsive computing environments.
