About Me

I am a PhD student at Inria and LIG laboratories. My research is part of the PULSE challenge—a collaboration between Inria, ADEME, and Qarnot computing—focused on hybridizing task scheduling and control theory in high-performance computing (HPC) systems.

My thesis, “Efficient task hybridization in heterogeneous computing: practical combinations of Control and Scheduling theories”, addresses the development of adaptive, robust controllers for resource-constrained, compute-intensive platforms.
